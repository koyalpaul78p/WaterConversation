Collect rainwater. Save our future.

🌧️

What is Rainwater Harvesting?

Rainwater harvesting is the process of collecting and storing rainwater for future use.

🏠 How Does It Work?

1. Rain falls on the rooftop.

2. Pipes carry the rainwater.

3. A filter removes dirt and debris.

4. Water is collected in a storage tank.

5. Stored water can be used for suitable non-drinking purposes.

🌿 Benefits

💧 Helps conserve water.

🌱 Supports gardening.

🏡 Can reduce dependence on other water sources.

🌍 Helps protect the environment.
